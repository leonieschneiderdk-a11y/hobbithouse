# How to walk around the hobbit house

There are two ways in. Pick the one that suits you.

| | **1. In your browser** | **2. In Blender (most realistic)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Looks | Good: scanned models and materials, bloom, sun shadows | Best: real light bouncing, soft shadows, reflections, glare on lamps |
| Setup | None | Install Blender once, double-click one file |
| Works on | Any PC, laptop or phone | A Windows, Mac or Linux PC (a graphics card helps a lot) |

---

## 1. In your browser

Open **https://leonieschneiderdk-a11y.github.io/hobbithouse/** and click **Enter the house**.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Walk |
| Mouse (click the view first) | Look around |
| Shift | Run |
| Space | Jump |
| M | Map: click anywhere to go there |
| **Graphics** button | Exposure, bloom (glow), sun height and direction, lamp brightness, haze, shadows |

The lift in the entrance hall takes you down to the garage: step in and stand still.

---

## 2. In Blender (the photoreal version)

### One-time setup

1. Install **Blender 4.2 or newer** (free): https://www.blender.org/download/
2. Download this project: on https://github.com/leonieschneiderdk-a11y/hobbithouse click **Code → Download ZIP**, then unzip it somewhere (for example your Desktop). Keep all the folders together; the house needs the `assets` folder next to it.

### Build the house (about 10–30 minutes, only needed once)

- **Windows:** double-click **`build_house_windows.bat`** in the unzipped folder. It finds Blender in `C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation`. If you installed Blender from Steam or the Microsoft Store, edit the `set BLENDER=` line in the file to point at your `blender.exe`.
- **Mac / Linux:** run `./build_house.sh` in a terminal from the unzipped folder.

The script builds every room, hallway, roof and the grounds, places all the scanned furniture, sets up the lighting, and saves **`hobbit_house.blend`**. Blender then opens it for you.

### Walk around

When Blender opens, you're looking through a camera standing in the entrance hall, already showing the real-time lit view.

1. Move the mouse over the big 3D view.
2. Press **Shift + `** (the key left of 1; on some keyboards it's **Shift + ~**) to start **Walk mode**.
3. Walk with **W A S D**, look with the mouse, **Shift** to go faster, **E / Q** to go up and down, **Tab** to toggle gravity (walk on the floor), and left-click to stop walking where you are.

If the view isn't showing the lit version, press **Z** over the 3D view and choose **Rendered**.

### Make it look even better

- **Settings for glow and glare:** these are already on (soft bloom around lamps and windows, and light streaks on bright lights). Change them in the **Compositor** tab at the top.
- **Turn the sun:** click the **sun** object in the list on the right and rotate it (**R**) to change the time of day.
- **Highest quality still image:** press **F12**. For the full photoreal look, switch **Render Engine** to **Cycles** on the right-hand panel (the camera icon tab) before pressing F12. It is slower but has true light bouncing.
- **Slow PC?** In Rendered view, lower **Render Properties → Sampling → Viewport** to 8, or use **Material Preview** (press **Z**) while walking.

### Change anything

The house is generated by `blender/build_scene.py`. Change a room in the floor plan in `index.html`, or the furniture rules in the script, and run the build again: the browser game and the Blender house both follow.
